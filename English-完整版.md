## Abstract

Unified field theory is discussed as if it were a single claim. It is not. We distinguish three claims that share the word "unification". Each is supported by different evidence, fails in a different way, and requires a different experiment to decide. Transformational unification, the claim that one field physically converts into another, is quantitatively falsified as an engineering proposition: the required field strengths exceed the Schwinger limit by an order of magnitude. Geometrical unification, the claim that known forces are projections of a higher-dimensional geometry, remains observationally open; its gravitational Aharonov-Bohm signatures are excluded at the electronvolt scale by orbital fifth-force bounds and remain testable only in the nanoelectronvolt window now reachable by the Th-229 nuclear clock. Algebraic unification, the claim that gauge theory and gravity share a deeper kinematic algebra, is an established mathematical fact that is silent on ontology. We organize these three claims, together with sixteen further falsifiable propositions from the quantum-gravity frontier, into a decision matrix in which every row carries a discriminating experiment, a target theory it would exclude, and a timescale. Sixteen of the nineteen rows are anchored by reproducible computations. The classification is descriptive, not predictive: it explains why a century of folk physics has failed at specific, quantifiable points, and it converts the vague ambition of "controlling gravity" into a map of testable statements.

## Introduction

The word "unified field theory" covers at least three logically distinct claims, and conflating them has produced a century of confusion. The first claim is transformational: that one field can be turned into another by some physical process, as when a changing electromagnetic field is said to generate gravity. The second claim is geometrical: that apparently different fields are projections of one higher-dimensional geometry, the route opened by Kaluza and Klein. The third claim is algebraic: that different theories share a deeper kinematic structure, the route now realized by the double copy program. These three claims fail differently. The first fails quantitatively, under elementary arithmetic. The second remains open, but its testable window is now known and narrow. The third is true as mathematics and undecidable as ontology.

This distinction matters beyond taxonomy. Folk physics, from Tesla's "Dynamic Theory of Gravity" to contemporary anti-gravity proposals, is almost always transformational in structure. Mainstream criticism usually answers with a blanket dismissal, which teaches nothing. A precise diagnosis requires identifying which unification claim is being made, and which specific arrow in the argument fails. We provide that diagnosis, and we show that the same discipline applies with equal force to the research frontier.

## Three unification claims, three evidential regimes

Transformational unification asserts a physical map from field A to field B, such that engineering A produces B as a matter of dynamics. Geometrical unification asserts an embedding of both A and B as components of one geometric object, with no claim that A produces B. Algebraic unification asserts a shared algebraic or kinematic structure from which the amplitudes of both theories can be obtained, again with no conversion involved. The empirical status of each claim differs in kind. Transformational unification is testable by direct computation and is falsified. Geometrical unification is testable only through its distinctive signatures, now constrained to a narrow window. Algebraic unification is not testable in the ordinary sense: no finite computation can decide whether the shared algebra is a deeper entity or a coincidence.

## Transformational unification: the quantitative verdict

A parallel-plate capacitor with a field of ten megavolts per meter, one cubic meter in volume, sources a gravitational acceleration of order 10⁻²⁵ m s⁻². Reversing the question: the field strength needed to produce Earth-scale gravity follows from E_needed = (2ρ/ε₀)^(1/2) with ρ = g c²/(8π G L). For g = 9.8 m s⁻² and L = 1 m, the required field is roughly eight times the Schwinger limit, at which the vacuum becomes unstable against pair production. At that point one is manufacturing matter, not engineering gravity. A Yukawa modification of Newtonian gravity, a_Y(r) = α(GM/r²)(1 + r/λ)e^(−r/λ), is excluded at order unity across all probed scales: roughly 3×10⁻³ at λ = 10 μm, 10⁻⁶ from millimeters to a hundred kilometers, and 10⁻⁹ to 10⁻¹⁵ at orbital scales. The only remaining opening lies below ten micrometers, where Casimir physics dominates.

## Geometrical unification: the open window is now known

An extra compact dimension contributes a Yukawa-type correction V(r) = −(Gm₁m₂/r)(1 + αe^(−r/λ)). The gravitational Aharonov-Bohm proposals convert this into a spectroscopic splitting ΔE = m_sys α ΔΦ(r,λ). Electronvolt-scale splittings claimed for nuclear systems require α of order 0.4; millielectronvolt splittings for atomic systems require α of order 10⁻³. Both are excluded by four to eight orders of magnitude by orbital bounds. The allowed window sits at the nanoelectronvolt scale, where the Th-229 nuclear clock is the natural instrument: 148 nm laser excitation, solid-state hosts with lifetimes exceeding six hundred seconds, and a spinless host crystal removing the dominant broadening channel are all in place. Geometrical unification is a specific measurement program with a known instrument and a known energy window.

## Algebraic unification: real mathematics, silent ontology

Off-shell N=8 supergravity is realized as the double copy of N=4 super Yang-Mills at cubic order. Amplitudes on coherent-state gauge backgrounds double-copy to amplitudes on curved spacetimes whose metric is built from the gauge background. The Ehlers transformation of general relativity is the double copy of electromagnetic duality. These are theorems of a well-defined algebraic structure. What they are not is evidence for a unified ontology: the residual gauge algebra of the double-copied Schwarzschild solution collapses to the finite isometry group. Shared algebraic structure does not imply shared physical content. Algebraic unification is real, and its ontological import is undecidable by computation alone.

## A criterion for folk theories: the four questions

Any candidate unified theory should answer four questions before its claims are evaluated: what tensor or algebraic object does it introduce, and how does it transform; can it be written as a covariant action; does it reproduce the verified limits; and what distinctive number does it predict? Applied to the folk tradition, this criterion locates the failure precisely: in the case most often cited, a correct kinematic relation (the Lorentz transformation of electromagnetic fields) is differentiated, and the acceleration term is then interpreted as a gravitational field. The first two steps are standard physics. The final identification is an extra interpretive step with no dynamical content. The failure is not that the mathematics is wrong; it is that the arrow is asserted rather than derived. The same discipline applies to the frontier: an observation discriminates between theories only if no competing theory reproduces it.

## The decision matrix as a map

Nineteen arrows, each carrying an evidence grade, a discriminating channel, a target it would exclude, and a timescale. Four are falsified (transformational conversion, expectation-valued sources of geometry, indefinite causal order as evidence for quantum spacetime, vacuum energy as an engineering resource). Two are established experimental facts (electromagnetic and electroweak unification). One is a classification statement of this paper (inertia engineering equals stress-energy engineering). Four are grade-A experimental facts. The remaining nine are live, bounded, and scheduled. The entanglement phase of the standard quantum-gravity-entanglement benchmark is φ = (Gm²t/ħ)(1/(d+Δx) + 1/(d−Δx) − 2/d), which evaluates to 0.217 radians for the benchmark parameters; realistic chip parameters give 10⁻⁶ to 10⁻⁵ radians, converting a two-hundred-measurement certification into a 10⁹-to-10²¹-measurement campaign. A free-floating one-kilogram apparatus at one kelvin caps the superposition separation of a 10⁹-atomic-mass-unit particle at roughly one hundred micrometers.

## Relation to existing literature

Three bodies of work are closest. First, the three-way classification itself: a systematic preprint search returned no prior proposal pairing unification types with the double copy; the nearest discussions are the unification-versus-reduction debates in the philosophy of physics, which this classification does not engage. Second, the warp-drive no-go literature: Bobrick and Martire showed that any warp drive is a shell of matter requiring propulsion; Le showed that steering requires radiation and mass loss; Barzegar, Buchert, and Vigneron classified warp-drive spacetimes and proved new no-go theorems. These are special cases of our control-channel classification; ours generalizes the logic to arbitrary engineering operations and adds the lever-efficacy corollary. Third, the term "metric engineering" itself, used by Puthoff in 2010 in an exploratory register; our verdict is stronger and specific.

## Outlook

The classification is descriptive, not predictive. Its content is diagnostic: it explains why transformational proposals fail at specific points, identifies the one open window for geometrical unification and its instrument, and states exactly what the double copy does and does not license. The question is no longer whether one field can be converted into another, but what variable, if any, controls spacetime at its source. The quantum state, the kinematic algebra, and the relational structure are the three live candidates, and each has a scheduled experiment. The map is the deliverable, and the map is falsifiable, which is all a scientific claim is allowed to be.

# Part II — The Tesla Question (condensed translation of Sections 1–28)

The paper opens not with a verdict but with an archaeology. Nikola Tesla did, in 1937, publicly claim a completed "Dynamic Theory of Gravity", and he did reject spacetime curvature in favor of a real force field and an ether. But no credible primary evidence exists that he published the theory in full, and none that it contained a "gravity door", a wormhole capture device, or a mechanism by which rotating electromagnetic fields reverse gravity. The folkloric apparatus around Tesla is a later construction.

The term "anti-gravity" itself decomposes into at least five distinct technologies: producing an ordinary upward force; changing inertial mass; changing gravitational mass; shielding an external field; and reshaping spacetime itself. Magnetic levitation is of the first kind and is real engineering. Gravity shields are of the fourth kind and exist only in fiction. Wormholes belong to the fifth. The chapter builds a five-level ladder (force, inertial, field, metric, quantum-state engineering) that later becomes the paper's central stack.

Three structural echoes between Tesla's intuition and modern physics survive scrutiny. Rotation really does "drag" spacetime: mass-energy angular momentum sources the gravitomagnetic field, measured by Gravity Probe B and now entering quantum-clock interferometry designs. Electromagnetic fields really do gravitate, through their full stress-energy content. And the ether question survives in altered form: the vacuum of quantum field theory is not empty, even though it is nothing like Tesla's medium. In each case the echo is structural, not technological; the chapter's discipline is that a structural similarity is not an inheritance claim.

The opening part closes by reframing the research question. The interesting object is not "did Tesla build anti-gravity", but "which degrees of freedom of physical reality could an advanced civilization engineer". That question is what the rest of the paper answers.

# Part III — What Unified Field Theory Has Verified (condensed translation of Sections 29–60)

Unification claims come in three layers. The first layer, that different forces can be described in one mathematical language, has succeeded completely. The second, that apparently different forces descend from one higher symmetry, is experimentally established for electromagnetism and the weak interaction through the electroweak theory. The third, that strong, electroweak, and gravitational interactions all descend from one fundamental structure, is experimentally unestablished. The accurate summary is that unification has succeeded halfway, and in a way unlike anything imagined in 1920: the keyword is symmetry, not medium.

Two completed unifications deserve attention as prototypes. Electricity and magnetism are components of one field tensor Fμν under Lorentz transformations; two apparent forces turned out to be projections of one higher-dimensional mathematical object. The electroweak theory repeats the pattern at a deeper level: photon, W±, and Z emerge from the broken symmetry SU(2)L × U(1)Y. The deeper message is that "different now" does not imply "different fundamentally": the universe may simply sit in a broken-symmetry low-energy state.

A stronger unifying framework replaces force with connection. In gauge theory the fundamental object is the connection Aμ, with field strength arising as its curvature; in general relativity the connection Γ determines parallel transport, with the Riemann curvature as its invariant. Both programs therefore converge on geometry, and a candidate like the 2025 Holomorphic Unified Field Theory, which recovers Einstein gravity, Maxwell, Yang-Mills, and chiral fermions from a four-complex-dimensional manifold, is best classified as an interesting candidate rather than a verified theory: recovering GR plus the Standard Model at low energy inherits their successes but does not verify its own distinctive structure. The evidence ladder stands as: electromagnetism and electroweak unification established; Yang-Mills description of strong and weak forces established; Higgs mechanism strongly supported; GUT-scale unification, gauge-gravity unification, quantum gravity, and a single fundamental object, all unestablished.

On the quantum side, matter-wave interference has now been demonstrated for sodium nanoparticles of more than seven thousand atoms and 170,000 Da, forming spatially delocalized Schrödinger-cat-like states. Macroscopicity by itself does not switch quantum mechanics off; the classical world plausibly arises from decoherence, environment, and complexity rather than from a mass threshold. This sharpens the central open question: a mass in a spatial superposition sources what gravitational field? General relativity answers with a definite spacetime; quantum mechanics answers with a superposition. The 2025 Nature debate over gravitationally induced entanglement, in which a classical-plus-matter construction was claimed to reproduce the effect and then rebutted, teaches that the field needs discriminating signatures, not merely interesting quantum effects.

Two reorganizations close this part. The first observes that every interaction, electromagnetic or gravitational, ultimately controls a quantum phase: Aμ couples to phase directly, and gμν couples through proper time. The common language of unification may be phase, not force. The second introduces the Reality Stack, seven layers from observables down to an unknown fundamental structure, and the Level A–D evidence discipline that the rest of the paper uses: A for experimental fact, B for mature theory in untested regimes, C for mathematically self-consistent candidates, and D for the paper's own extrapolations. Within that discipline the central hypothesis can be stated safely: gravity is probably the wrong engineering variable.

# Part IV — Mass, Inertia, and Tμν (condensed translation of Sections 69–75)

Mass keeps three account books. The first belongs to the Higgs: W and Z masses through the Brout-Englert-Higgs mechanism, fermion masses through Yukawa couplings. The second belongs to QCD: lattice decompositions of the proton mass give roughly 9% to the quark mass term, 32–33% to quark kinetic energy, 36–37% to gluon field energy, and 23% to the trace anomaly. The Higgs seed of about one percent of the proton mass is amplified ninefold by the chiral condensate, and the remaining ninety-one percent is dynamics. The third book belongs to general relativity, which reads only the total stress-energy tensor and is indifferent to the decomposition.

The chain became experimental science in 2024–2026. The J/ψ-007 collaboration measured the proton's gluonic gravitational form factor with lattice-comparable precision; a reconstruction path for the gluon trace anomaly now exists through three observables; an in-orbit atom-interferometry test of the weak equivalence principle on the China Space Station reached an uncertainty of 2.8×10⁻⁸; and proposals now treat inertial and gravitational mass as operators, making the variance of the acceleration operator a new WEP channel.

Inertia's philosophical question has a precise modern status. General relativity implements Mach's principle in the sense that inertial frames are determined by matter through the field equations; the quantitative Sciama-style version, in which distant matter directly induces local inertia, gives a coefficient k < 1 that depends on cosmological parameters, so the strong Machian claim is at best partially realized. A toy model in fracton physics shows the mechanism can be made computable, but the strong claim remains speculative.

The most unexpected structural discovery of this part is a three-way analogy: the pressure-energy equation of state of the nucleon, of type-II superconductor vortices, and of ΛCDM cosmology share one structure, the static pressure equal to minus the trace energy density, each arising from the depletion of a condensate (gluon and quark condensates, Cooper pairs, the cosmological constant). Confinement, superconductivity, and cosmic acceleration are three faces of one trace structure. This is the paper's third example of structural, rather than transformational, unification.

The vacuum, finally, is not an engineering resource in any well-defined sense. The same free scalar theory yields +Λ⁴ under a cutoff, zero under zeta-function regularization, and ±m⁴ln(m²/μ²) under dimensional regularization, with the sign flipped by the renormalization scale. Only differences of vacuum energy, the Casimir type, are scheme-independent and measurable. And the two forms of "mass engineering" humanity has actually achieved, superconductivity and negative effective mass in metamaterials, both demonstrate the same lesson: emergent or effective mass does not change the form of gravitational coupling. Mass engineering is not gravity engineering. The verdict of this part is the paper's strongest theorem in prose: within GR plus the Standard Model, inertia engineering has no degrees of freedom independent of stress-energy engineering.

# Part V — The Engineering Boundary of Physical Reality (condensed translation of Sections 85–90)

Seven computations, run after the roadmap was drafted, changed five of its rows. The GIE difficulty acquired two numbers: 0.217 radians for the benchmark versus 10⁻⁶–10⁻⁵ radians for realistic chip parameters, converting a two-hundred-run certification into a 10⁹-to-10²¹-run campaign. The apparatus-recoil bound became concrete: a free-floating kilogram apparatus at one kelvin caps the superposition of a 10⁹-atomic-mass-unit particle near one hundred micrometers. The geontropic model's falsifiable window survived only at the strong scale: 0.03 seconds versus 10³³ seconds. The gravitational Aharonov-Bohm effect turned out to equal the classical redshift variation numerically, so the experiment discriminates interpretations, not general relativity. And the Kaluza-Klein electronvolt coincidence closed: the required couplings are excluded by four to eight orders of magnitude, leaving the nanoelectronvolt window to the nuclear clock.

The final decision matrix therefore stands at nineteen rows: four falsified, two established experimental facts, one classification statement of this paper, four grade-A facts, and nine live B/C-grade rows, every one carrying a discriminating channel, a target it would exclude, and a timescale. The central proposition now has three layers. Historically, the transformational unification of Tesla and Zhang has no experimental basis, but their question, whether a unified dynamical structure lies behind the world, is the real question of fundamental physics. Physically, gravity and inertia are the wrong engineering variables; the only entry point is Tμν, whose correct engineering name is energy engineering. At the frontier, Tμν itself may be derived, with the quantum state, the kinematic algebra, and the relational structure as the three live candidates for its source, each with a scheduled experiment.

Twelve action items, sorted by timescale, complete the map: five near-term (GQuEST data, reservoir-engineered GIE, WEP systematic budgets, nuclear clock stability, apparatus-recoil verification), four mid-term (first QGEM rounds, discriminating Aharonov-Bohm geometries, 10⁻¹⁷ WEP, a MAQRO-class mission), and three far-term (frame-dragging entanglement, bolometric graviton noise, quantum causal structure). The final answer to the Tesla question has not changed in its first part, no credible evidence, but its third part has: the question Tesla could not stop asking is the question the frontier is now scheduled to answer. If anti-gravity ever exists, it will not be a machine that resists gravity. It will be the understanding and control of the degrees of freedom that produce inertia, time, distance, and causal structure, and the first step of that road is already drawn: nineteen rows, sixteen scripts, all reproducible.

# Part VI — Formal Apparatus (from the submission blueprint)

## The control-channel uniqueness theorem

Let (M, g) be a general-relativistic spacetime with g determined by given sources and boundary conditions. Let E be an engineering operation satisfying: (A1) all field sources are unchanged; (A2) the metric is not replaced by a solution not determined by those sources and boundary conditions; (A3) no beyond-Standard-Model channel is introduced. Then E leaves all test-particle geodesic motion unchanged. The theorem is a classification statement: (A1)–(A3) exhaust the control channels available inside GR plus the Standard Model. Violating A1 is stress-energy engineering, the only game in town. Violating A2 is metric engineering, requiring exotic matter or modified gravity. Violating A3 is new physics, bounded by fifth-force and Lorentz-violation searches.

Corollary (mass-lever efficacy): for any decomposition of mass m = m_Higgs + m_QCD + m_vac + …, the gravitational efficacy of each component as an engineering lever equals ∂Tμν/∂(that component). The Higgs enters about nine percent of the proton mass term; QCD carries about ninety-one percent; "vacuum engineering" is not well-defined, since the vacuum contribution to Tμν depends on the regularization scheme. The theorem is deliberately bounded: it holds for classical general relativity with classical sources. Quantum superposed sources, emergent-geometry hypotheses, and hybrid classical-quantum models lie outside it, and are precisely the open rows of the decision matrix.
